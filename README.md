## 📊 Project Overview

The TechnoEdge Sales Analysis project is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, and business trends.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights that can help understand overall business performance and identify high-performing products, customers, categories, and segments.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## 📈 Key KPIs

- Total Customers: 109
- Total Quantity: 977
- Total Sales: 57,438.86
- Net Profit: 14,408.92

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Product Analysis
- Top 5 selling products
- Bottom 5 selling products
- Top 5 most profitable products
- Product-level performance analysis

### Sales Analysis
- Sales by product category
- Total Sales vs Total Profit by year
- Net Profit by date
- Category-wise orders

### Customer Analysis
- Customer-level profit analysis
- Top customer identification
- Customer performance across countries

### Segment Analysis
- Sales percentage by segment
- Comparison of sales and profit across segments
- Analysis of End-user, Company, and Home Office segments

## 💡 Key Insights

- End-user segment contributes the highest share of sales at 62.97%.
- Company contributes 19.83% and Home Office contributes 17.20%.
- Panasonic is the most profitable product among the products shown in the dashboard.
- The dashboard identifies BoomBox as the top-selling product among the displayed Top 5.
- The highest sale shown in the report occurred on August 9, 2020.
- Becky Martin is identified as the top customer in the dashboard.
